## 🏗️ Active Initiatives

**🤖 SIH 2026 | Team VisionGrid** &nbsp;![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Competition_in_Progress-b7410e?style=flat-square)

VisionGrid is a city-wide AI platform that connects number plate detections from multiple cameras into a single picture of how a vehicle moves through a city. Existing ANPR systems treat each camera in isolation. Our approach links sightings using plate text, camera location, and timestamps to reconstruct vehicle routes, and a map-based dashboard lets users search a plate and view its journey alongside traffic density and heatmap analytics. It is designed around existing camera infrastructure, and our YOLOv8 plate detector reached an mAP@0.5 of 0.994 during development. The idea has been submitted to the Smart India Hackathon 2026 (Problem Statement 26127), and the competition is currently in progress.

<br>

**🔬 UGRP | Student Feedback Sentiment Analyser** &nbsp;![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Ongoing_Research-b7410e?style=flat-square)

Undergraduate Research Project at Ahmedabad University. Comparative study of rule-based (VADER) and transformer-based (RoBERTa) sentiment models on academic feedback data. Abstract submitted to ResCon, IIT Bombay. Supervised by faculty at SEAS.
